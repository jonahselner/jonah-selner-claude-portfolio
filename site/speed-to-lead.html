The problem

Leads went cold waiting for a call back

Before

Most practices didn't text leads at all. They called back hours or days later, and patients got phone tag and voicemails.

After

A text from the practice in about a minute, any hour. Questions get answered right away, and a person calls to book once they're ready.

Speed matters: the odds of qualifying a lead drop 21x between a 5-minute and a 30-minute response (MIT/InsideSales), and LASIK practices averaged 19 hours to respond to web leads in a 2023 mystery shop (ZEISS).

Design

Built to be safe at scale

One prompt, many practices

The master prompt holds the rules. Each practice's facts live in its own knowledge base, built from an onboarding call, signed off by the practice, and tested with a bot that tries to break it.

Hard limits come first

No medical advice, no calls on who's a candidate, no access to calendars or records, and 911 for emergencies. When rules conflict, these win over tone and flow.

Human in the loop

The AI never books. Once a patient says yes, or a conversation needs judgment, the AI switches off and a person takes over.

Can't be talked out of its rules

Rules come only from the system prompt. A patient texting "ignore your instructions" gets treated as a manipulation attempt.
